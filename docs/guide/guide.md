# Restoration Workshop: step guide

One section per object (one tab each in the Google Doc). Each step has a picture of the object, the tool and one line on what the tool does.

## Rusty Padlock

### Step 1: Chip Off the Rust Scale

Chip the rust lumps off the lock.

![Chip Off the Rust Scale](img/padlock/01-chip.jpg)
![tool](img/tools/chisel.jpg)

**Chisel: knocks off crusty rust and splits seams.**

### Step 2: Electrolysis Bath

Electrolysis bath lifts the remaining rust.

![Electrolysis Bath](img/padlock/02-electro.jpg)

**Cleaning tank: the bath the object is lowered into.**

### Step 3: Free the Stuck Key

Heat the keyhole, then twist out the stuck key.

![Free the Stuck Key](img/padlock/03-key.jpg)
![tool](img/tools/torch.jpg)

**Butane torch: heats metal until it glows so it can be freed, bent or brazed.**

### Step 4: Split the Body Open

Split the lock body open along the seam.

![Split the Body Open](img/padlock/04-split.jpg)
![tool](img/tools/chisel.jpg)

**Chisel: knocks off crusty rust and splits seams.**

### Step 5: Wire-Wheel to Bright Steel

Wire-wheel every part to bright steel.

![Wire-Wheel to Bright Steel](img/padlock/05-wheel.jpg)
![tool](img/tools/grind.jpg)

**Rotary grinder: wire wheel, burr or sanding disc that takes metal back to bright steel.**

### Step 6: Cold-Blue the Iron

Brush on cold bluing for a blue-black finish.

![Cold-Blue the Iron](img/padlock/06-blue.jpg)
![tool](img/tools/stain.jpg)

**Stain brush: brushes on liquids such as bluing, oil, stain or stripper.**

### Step 7: Refit the Lock Parts

Put the levers, shackle and plate back.

![Refit the Lock Parts](img/padlock/07-refit.jpg)

**Hands: drag, lift and fit parts by hand.**

### Step 8: Peen the Rivets

Peen the rivets shut, timing each strike.

![Peen the Rivets](img/padlock/08-rivets.jpg)
![tool](img/tools/hammer.jpg)

**Hammer: drives out and peens rivets.**

### Step 9: Key Test

Turn the key; the shackle springs open.

![Key Test](img/padlock/09-test.jpg)

**Hands: drag, lift and fit parts by hand.**
