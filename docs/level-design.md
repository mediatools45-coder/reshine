# Level redesign from the restoration videos

Source: `docs/video-steps.md` (245 steps across 6 videos). Rules:
- 8–10 steps per level.
- No step repeats an action within its level (e.g. only one bath, one wire-brush pass, one paint coat).
- Filler is dropped: finding the object, unboxing, laying parts out, drying and waiting, repeated disassembly.
- Each level keeps the video's best moment and adds at least one new mechanic.
- Every level ends with the before/after slider.

`NEW` marks a mechanic the game doesn't have yet.

## 1. Rusty Padlock (19th-century riveted lock, key rusted in place)
| # | Step | Mechanic | Video |
|---|------|----------|-------|
| 1 | Chip off the rust scale | Tap/scratch crust chunks with a chisel | 0:40 |
| 2 | Electrolysis bath | Clip the red/black leads, lower the lock in, then swirl until the sludge rises | 2:20–4:14 |
| 3 | Free the stuck key | **NEW** heat: hold the torch until the keyhole glows, then twist the key out against resistance | 5:09–5:29 |
| 4 | Split the body open | Hammer the chisel along the seam (rhythm taps) | 10:11 |
| 5 | Wire-wheel to bright steel | Scrub with the bench wire wheel | 8:05 |
| 6 | Cold-blue the parts | Brush on bluing, then rub the high spots back to silver | 15:38–15:43 |
| 7 | Refit the levers & spring | Drag the parts into their slots | 17:29 |
| 8 | Peen the rivets shut | **NEW** timing: strike when the marker is in the sweet spot | 18:27 |
| 9 | Key test (+ before/after) | Turn the original key; the shackle springs open and snaps shut | 19:26 ★ |

## Levels without a video (Sword, Lighter, Watch, Pistol)
Same rules apply: cut each to 8–10 steps, remove repeated cleaning passes, and add one or two of the new mechanics (timing strike, heat, tuning, laser engraving). Those step lists come after the six above are approved.
