# Restoration videos: step-by-step breakdown

These steps come from six restoration videos. For each video I pulled a frame every 5–15 s, plus one at every scene change (HSV-histogram cut detection), and reviewed them as timestamped contact sheets. Timestamps are mm:ss into the video file and are accurate to the sampling interval (±5–15 s). Tool names come from what is visible on screen and from on-screen captions; none of the videos has narration I could check.

| # | Video | Length | Steps |
|---|-------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Compaq keyboard ("hasn't been white in 25 years") | 8:03 | 22 |
| 2 | Fender Stratocaster (guitar segment of a compilation) | 16:22 of 42:27 | 46 |
| 3 | Olympia Progress typewriter, 1949 | 28:08 | 56 |
| 4 | Mechanical alarm clock, 1950 | 21:21 | 47 |
| 5 | 105-year-old pocket knife | 12:54 | 34 |
